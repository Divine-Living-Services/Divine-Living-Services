Community Access

Get Out, Get Involved, Stay Connected

Being part of your community matters. We support you to attend social events, join local programs, run errands, or simply enjoy time outside the home — safely and confidently.

What We Do

  • Accompany you to social, recreational, and community activities
  • Support you to build friendships and community connections
  • Help with transport to appointments, shops, or events
  • Encourage participation in programs that match your interests and goals

Who This Is For

Anyone wanting more independence, social connection, and confidence getting out into the community.
