About Us

Care That Comes From the Heart — and the Right Qualifications

Divine Living Services was founded on a simple belief: every person deserves support that helps them live life on their own terms — not someone else's idea of what that should look like.

We started this business because we saw too many people receiving support that felt rushed, impersonal, or disconnected from what they actually needed. We wanted to do it differently — with genuine care, consistency, and respect at the centre of everything.

Today, we support NDIS participants across Melbourne's South-Eastern and Western suburbs with Support Coordination, Community Access, Daily Living, and Nursing services. But no matter how much we grow, our approach stays the same: listen first, support second, and never lose sight of the person behind the plan.

Our Values

  • Dignity, always. You're a person first, not a set of goals on a page.
  • Reliability. We show up, on time, every time.
  • Transparency. Clear communication with you, your family, and your Support Coordinator.
  • Cultural respect. We take the time to understand and honour your background, beliefs, and preferences.

Our Team

Our support workers and nurses are qualified, experienced, and — just as importantly — chosen for their compassion. Every team member undergoes NDIS Worker Screening, ongoing training, and regular performance reviews, so you can feel confident in who's supporting you or your loved one.
