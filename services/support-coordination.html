Support Coordination

Making Your NDIS Plan Actually Work for You

Your NDIS plan is full of potential — but turning it into real, useful support can feel overwhelming. That's where we come in.

What We Do

  • Help you understand your funding and what it can be used for
  • Connect you with trustworthy service providers
  • Coordinate multiple supports so they work together, not against each other
  • Build your skills and confidence to manage more independently over time
  • Step in when things go wrong and help resolve issues quickly

Approved NDIS Supports We Deliver

  • 0106 — Assistance in Coordinating or Managing Life Stages, Transitions and Supports. Helping you navigate changes in your life — finishing school, moving out of home, changing providers, or adjusting to a new diagnosis — while keeping your supports connected and working together.

Who This Is For

Participants who are new to the NDIS, navigating a complex plan, or who simply want an experienced hand guiding the way.
